Tina and I rode our bikes from Astoria to Crescent City, California during a
week long vacation.  We met a few people and ended up riding along with Layla and Jeff for a few days.

I went overboard and took around 300 pictures on my Canon, so here is just a smattering of them, with a slant towards sunsets at Brookings.
